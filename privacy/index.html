Privacy Policy

What we collect when you use this site or request a quote, and how we use it.

What we collect

When you request a quote, we collect what you enter in the form: your name, phone number, email (if given), service location, vehicle details, the services and add-ons you pick, any notes, and any photos you upload.

Like most websites, we also collect basic usage data automatically, such as the pages you visit, your device and browser type, and how you found the site. This is gathered through cookies and similar tools described below.

How we use it

  • To reply with your quote and schedule your detail
  • To contact you by phone, text or email about your request and service
  • To understand how people use the site and to measure and improve our ads

Text messages

If you check the consent box on the quote form, we may text you about your quote, scheduling and requested services. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out at any time, or HELP for help.

We do not sell or share your mobile number or text consent with third parties for their marketing.

Who we share it with

We do not sell your personal information. We only share it with services that help us run the site and the business:

  • FormSubmit delivers quote form submissions to our email.
  • Cloudflare hosts this website.
  • Google Analytics tells us how visitors use the site.
  • Meta (Facebook and Instagram) may receive site activity through the Meta Pixel so we can measure and show our ads.

We may also share information if required by law.

Cookies and ads

Google Analytics and the Meta Pixel use cookies and similar technology to collect information about your visit. Meta and other companies may use this to show you ads on other sites and apps. You can control this by:

  • Blocking or deleting cookies in your browser settings
  • Installing the Google Analytics opt-out add-on
  • Changing your Facebook ad preferences
  • Opting out at aboutads.info

How long we keep it

We keep quote and customer information as long as we need it to provide service and keep normal business records, then delete it.

Your choices

You can ask us to see, correct or delete the information we have about you. Just call or text us at the number below.

Children

This site is not meant for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information.

Changes

If we update this policy, we will change the date at the top of this page.
