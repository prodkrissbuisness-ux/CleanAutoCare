Questions

Quick answers

Do you come to me?

Yes. We detail at your home, work or another approved location.

How much does it cost?

Price depends on three things: the service you choose, the size of your vehicle and how dirty it is. The prices below are for a basic detail.

Starting prices by vehicle size
Coupe or compact: exterior $75, interior $100, full detail $150
Sedan: exterior $100, interior $125, full detail $175
SUV: exterior $100, interior $150, full detail $200
Truck: exterior $120, interior $125–$150, full detail $230

Condition adjustment
Light: no extra charge. Moderate: +$25 to $50. Heavy: +$50 to $100. Very dirty, with pet hair or stains: +$75 to $150. The exact amount depends on your vehicle size.

Add-ons and deep cleans
Pet hair removal ($30–$60), heavy stain treatment ($60–$100), headlight restoration ($50) and ceramic paint protection ($150–$300) are priced separately. Deep cleans are quoted individually after we see photos of the vehicle.

The quote builder gives you an estimate in about a minute, and no deposit or payment is collected online. Pet hair, stains, add-ons or travel may change the final price, and we confirm it with you before we start.

Can you remove pet hair and stains?

Yes. Send photos with your quote request and we will plan the work.
