About

About Clean Auto Care

Clean Auto Care is a mobile detailing service based in Baraboo and serving Central Wisconsin including Sauk, Reedsburg, Portage, and Wisconsin Dells. We specialize in interior detailing, exterior washes, deep cleaning, stain removal, odor treatment, and paint protection.

Our goal is simple — provide high quality detailing with honest pricing, attention to detail, and convenient mobile service that comes directly to you.

Whether your vehicle needs a maintenance clean or a full interior reset, Clean Auto Care focuses on making vehicles feel fresh, clean, and well maintained again.
