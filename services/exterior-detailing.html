Mobile exterior care for Wisconsin vehicles

Road film, dust, insects and changing Wisconsin seasons can quickly dull a vehicle's exterior. Clean Auto Care provides mobile exterior detailing in Baraboo and surrounding communities, helping drivers maintain a cleaner finish without taking the vehicle to a shop.

We use a careful hand-wash process and give focused attention to the wheels, tires and exterior surfaces that define the final result.

What exterior detailing includes

  • Safe exterior hand wash
  • Wheel and tire cleaning
  • Tire shine
  • Exterior trim attention
  • Available paint-protection application

Service recommendations depend on the vehicle's current condition. Share photos when requesting a quote so we can identify heavy contamination or other needs before the appointment.

Paint protection add-on

An available ceramic paint-protection option can add short-term protection after the exterior is cleaned. The listed six-week ceramic paint-protection add-on ranges from $150 to $300 based on vehicle size and condition.

Pair exterior and interior detailing

A full detail combines interior cleaning with exterior service for an inside-and-out reset. Full details start at $150, with the final price based on vehicle size, condition and requested add-ons.

Exterior detailing near Baraboo

Mobile appointments are available in Baraboo, Reedsburg, Wisconsin Dells, Portage and communities across Sauk County. Include your exact location so we can confirm travel availability.

Exterior detailing FAQs

Is an exterior detail the same as an automatic car wash?

No. Clean Auto Care provides a hands-on mobile service with focused wheel, tire, trim and finish care rather than a drive-through wash.

Can I book an inside-and-out detail?

Yes. The full-detail package combines interior detailing, an exterior wash, tire shine and applied paint protection.

Do you detail trucks and SUVs?

Yes. We work on cars, trucks and SUVs. Larger vehicles may require a higher quote because of the additional surface area and time.
