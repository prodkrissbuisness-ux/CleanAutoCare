When a standard detail is not enough

Some vehicles need more time and targeted work than a regular interior detail allows. The Reset is for heavily used interiors, embedded pet hair, visible stains, spills, odors and real buildup.

Every heavy-condition vehicle is different, so The Reset is quoted one vehicle at a time. Clear photos let us plan the work and give you a set price before we arrive.

What we can take on

  • Pet hair embedded in carpet and upholstery
  • Heavy or widespread stains
  • Spills and sticky buildup
  • Odors
  • Neglected floors, seats, panels and storage areas

Results depend on the material, its age and how severe the problem is. We set expectations before the appointment. We do not take on mold.

Pet hair and stains

On a regular detail, pet hair removal runs $50 to $100+ and stain treatment $25 to $100+. Bigger jobs are folded into one Reset price.

How it works

  1. Send a request and pick The Reset.
  2. We call or text. Send a few photos of the seats, floors, cargo area and problem spots.
  3. You get a set, itemized price before anything is booked.

Deep-clean questions

How much does a deep clean cost?

It depends on the time and treatment needed, so it is priced after photos. You know the total before you book.

Can every stain or odor be removed?

Not always. Material, age and severity affect the result, and we will tell you what to expect up front.
