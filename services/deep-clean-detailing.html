When a standard interior detail is not enough

Some vehicles need more time and targeted work than a maintenance cleaning allows. Clean Auto Care's deep-clean detailing is designed for heavily used interiors, pet hair, visible staining, spills, odors and areas with significant buildup.

Because every heavy-condition vehicle is different, deep-clean packages are quoted individually. Clear photos help us understand the starting condition and recommend the right approach before arriving.

Deep-clean needs we can address

  • Pet hair embedded in carpet and upholstery
  • Heavy or widespread stains
  • Spills and sticky buildup
  • Odor-treatment needs
  • Neglected floors, seats, panels and storage areas

Results depend on the material, age and severity of contamination. We will discuss the service plan and quote before the appointment so expectations are clear.

Pet hair and stain add-ons

Pet hair removal is listed at $30–$60, while heavy stain treatment is listed at $60–$100. Larger or more severe jobs may be included in a broader custom quote rather than treated as a simple add-on.

How to request an accurate deep-clean quote

  1. Share your vehicle's year, make, model and size.
  2. Describe pet hair, stains, odors, spills and any especially affected areas.
  3. Upload clear photos of the seats, floors, cargo area and problem spots.
  4. Include the service location so travel availability can be confirmed.

Mobile deep cleaning across the Baraboo area

We serve Baraboo, Reedsburg, Wisconsin Dells, Portage and communities throughout Sauk County. Appointments outside Baraboo depend on the address and schedule.

Deep-clean detailing FAQs

How much does a deep-clean detail cost?

Deep cleans receive a custom quote because the time and treatment required vary considerably. Photos are the best way to start.

Can every stain or odor be fully removed?

Not every stain or odor is guaranteed to disappear. Material type, age and contamination severity affect the result. We set expectations after reviewing the vehicle.

Can I add exterior detailing?

Yes. Ask about combining deep interior work with exterior detailing for a more complete reset.
