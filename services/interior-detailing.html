A thorough clean for the whole cabin

Wisconsin weather, daily commutes, kids, pets and weekend travel all leave their mark inside a vehicle. Clean Auto Care brings interior car detailing to your location in Baraboo and nearby communities so your cabin can feel clean and comfortable again.

Our standard interior detail covers the areas drivers touch and see most, along with the floors, seats and hard-to-reach spaces that collect dust and debris.

What an interior detail includes

  • Thorough vacuuming of floors, seats and accessible crevices
  • Cleaning of dashboards, consoles, cupholders, door panels and vents
  • Carpet, upholstery and leather cleaning appropriate to the material
  • Interior window and mirror cleaning
  • Attention to commonly missed edges and storage areas

Vehicle condition varies, so the final service plan and price are confirmed before work begins. Pet hair, heavy staining, odors and extreme buildup may require a deep-clean quote.

Interior detailing for cars, trucks and SUVs

Compact cars, family SUVs and work trucks all have different layouts and cleaning needs. Tell us your vehicle's year, make, model and size when requesting a quote. Clear photos of the seats, floors and problem areas help us estimate the job accurately.

Serving Baraboo and Central Wisconsin

We provide mobile interior auto detailing in Baraboo, Reedsburg, Wisconsin Dells, Portage and communities throughout Sauk County. Travel availability depends on the exact location and appointment, so include your address or city with your request.

Interior detailing FAQs

Yes. Interior packages include cleaning for the vehicle's applicable carpet, upholstery and leather surfaces.

Can you handle pet hair or heavy stains?

Yes. Pet hair removal and heavy stain treatment are available as add-ons or as part of a custom deep-clean quote.
