A thorough clean for the whole cabin

Wisconsin weather, commutes, kids, pets and weekend trips all leave their mark inside a vehicle. Clean Auto Care brings interior detailing to your home or work in Baraboo and nearby communities.

What The Interior includes

  • Thorough vacuuming of floors, seats and reachable crevices
  • Dashboard, console, cupholders, door panels and vents
  • Carpet, upholstery and leather cleaning suited to the material
  • Interior windows and mirrors
  • The edges and storage spots that usually get missed

Every vehicle is different, so we confirm an itemized price with you before booking. Embedded pet hair and stain treatment are add-ons, and years of buildup is quoted as The Reset.

Cars, trucks and SUVs

Compacts, family SUVs and work trucks all take different amounts of time. Pick your vehicle when you send a request and we will talk through the rest.

Interior detailing questions

How much does an interior detail cost?

The Interior starts at $150 for a coupe in light condition. Larger vehicles, heavier dirt, pet hair and stains add to it, and you see every line before you book.

Do you clean leather and upholstery?

Yes. Carpet, upholstery and leather are all included.

Can you handle pet hair or stains?

Yes. Pet hair removal starts at $50 and stain treatment at $25.
