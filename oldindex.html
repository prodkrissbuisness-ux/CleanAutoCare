Frequently asked questions

Mobile detailing questions

Do you come to my home in Baraboo?

Yes. Clean Auto Care is a mobile detailing service based in Baraboo. We come to your home or another approved service location after confirming the appointment details.

What areas outside Baraboo do you serve?

Our service area includes Reedsburg, Wisconsin Dells, Portage and communities across Sauk County. Availability varies by exact address, so send your location when requesting a quote.

How much does mobile auto detailing cost?

Interior details start at $100 and full details start at $150. Deep cleaning and add-ons are quoted according to vehicle size, condition, stains, pet hair and other factors.

Can you remove pet hair and stains?

Yes. Pet hair removal and heavy stain treatment are available. Photos help us estimate the time and level of cleaning required before your appointment.

How do I get the most accurate quote?

Send the vehicle year, make, model and size, describe its condition, select the service you want and include clear photos of problem areas.
